LogVita
LogVita is a privacy-first personal health record (PHR) management platform.
 * Bring Your Own Storage (BYOS): Health records and photos are stored directly in your personal Google Drive or Dropbox, giving you full ownership of your data.
 * End-to-End Encryption (E2EE): Sensitive data is encrypted in the browser via AES-GCM before uploading, ensuring safe transmission and storage.
 * Granular Sharing Control: Securely share records with family members or healthcare providers with customizable access scopes and expiration dates.
 * Emergency Health ID Card: Visualizes critical health details such as blood type, allergy history, and emergency contacts.
 * Multi-Language Support: Available in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean.
